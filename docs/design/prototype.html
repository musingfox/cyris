Design language

One system, two densities

The digest is the reference. Archive, digest and raw read at a loose density; the raw page's triage view and settings operate at a tight one. Same tokens, same components: rounded controls inside square surfaces. Browse the pages from the bar above.

Rules
Round controls, square surfaces

Buttons, inputs, segmented controls take 6px; tags are fully round. Panels, cards, tables and notices stay square.

Layers, not shadows

Depth comes from bg → bg-elev → surface → surface-2 and a 1px border.

One label voice

Geist Mono, uppercase in CSS, 0.1em tracking. Navigation, tabs, buttons, field names.

Two meanings of colour

Green is the main action, the selection, a good result. Pink is destructive or an error. Neutral outcomes stay grey.
