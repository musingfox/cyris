CYRIS

Put your attention on what really matters.

How it works

  1. Cyris reads the feeds and newsletters you subscribe to and writes one digest from them.

  2. You give each source a lane, and the lane decides what Cyris does with its articles.

    read
    Each article is summarized and scored.
    follow
    Each article comes through as written and is never scored.
    skim
    Articles are filtered, and news on one topic is grouped together.

  3. The lanes you chose set each article's layer.

    Stories
    Each read or follow article gets its own row, with its image, its summary or excerpt, and its source.
    Topics
    A news topic with two or more articles becomes one card with an image, a short excerpt and a link to each of them.
    Headlines
    A topic with only one article, or a filtered headline, becomes a single line. What the filter leaves out stays on the full list.

  4. You read it one layer at a time: Stories first, then Topics, then Headlines.

  5. Your up and down votes can let later issues skip articles like the ones you turned down.

  6. It arrives by email, or as a Discord message that links to the new issue.

Run it your way: on your computer, in your own Cloudflare account, or hosted, which is coming soon.

  • On your computer

    docker compose or uv

    Runs on your own machine and fetches your RSS feeds every hour by itself. Free, apart from your LLM key's usage. Issue pages have no vote buttons here, so you vote in the setup wizard or from the command line.

  • Your Cloudflare account Paid plan

    deployed to your own account

    Makes and publishes each issue on schedule, without your computer. Its container needs the Workers Paid plan. Voting on an issue page also needs the optional vote Worker and a sign-in.
